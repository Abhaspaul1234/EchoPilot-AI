Motorsport Speech-to-Text

High-fidelity transcription tuned for noisy cockpit environments. Overcomes wind draft, engine pitch, transmission static, and driver breath patterns.

Tone & Emotion Engine

Deep frequency evaluation of voice registers. Detects fatigue (tired), agitation (stressed), and calmness directly through pitch variations and structural word rates.

Telemetry Correlation

Aligns physiological vocal markers directly onto lap-time telemetry charts. Highlights exact track locations and corners where verbal anxiety correlates with time loss.

Race Pit Wall Insights

Synthesizes contextual voice-to-action suggestions. Recommends adjustment strategies for driver focus, pacing intervals, or cockpit coaching to maximize tires.
