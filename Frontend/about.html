THE PROBLEM // INVISIBLE STRUGGLE

Standard racing telemetry maps every hardware component of the race car: throttle travel, tyre surface temperature, shock displacement, and engine cylinder pressures. However, the most critical element of the vehicle assembly—the driver—is largely unmapped.

Vocal stress, cognitive overload, and extreme physical fatigue under high G-forces degrade reaction delta long before tyre wear patterns reflect it in telemetry tables. By ignoring biometrics, race engineers miss early indicators of driver panic or communication degradation, resulting in suboptimal pit wall strategies.

PROJECT VISION // ECHOPILOT AI

We created EchoPilot AI to parse driver radio communications as biometric telemetry. By extracting high-noise speech frequencies, detecting vocal stress registers, and correlating them directly onto lap-time graphs, we bridge the gap between engineering sensors and driver acoustics.

HOW ECHOPILOT WORKS // PIPELINE STEPS

01

Ingest & Filter

Ingests driver cockpit voice files (.wav, .mp3) alongside lap-times (.csv). Filters high-pitched engine notes and wind static through an acoustic noise-reduction filter.

02

Speech & Tone Scan

Transcribes engine communications and evaluates pitch frequency variance. Identifies stress markers based on sentence length ratios and verbal agitation curves.

03

Telemetry Overlay

Synchronizes transcript event timestamps with telemetry lap arrays, graphing stress indexes against throttle deltas to identify performance loss zones.
